Questions

Before you give

Where does my gift go?

To patrol, K-9 and dispatch operations in Zone 1, Pico-Robertson and Beverlywood, and to the vehicles, communications and equipment those teams need. Each dollar traces to an hour of coverage.

Is JDF a private security company?

No. JDF is a public safety nonprofit. Nobody signs a contract and nobody receives an invoice. Armed response is provided through a licensed California Private Patrol Operator.

Does JDF replace the police?

No. 911 comes first. Every criminal matter is escalated immediately to 911 and LAPD. JDF complements law enforcement and never replaces it.

Can I give anonymously?

Yes. Tick "Keep my gift anonymous" and your name will never be listed publicly. We still need an email address to send your receipt.

Can I dedicate my gift?

Yes. Choose "In honor of" or "In memory of" and add a name. It will appear on your receipt.
