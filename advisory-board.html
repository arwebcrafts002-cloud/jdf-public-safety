What Our Board Does

GUIDING OUR MISSION

The Security Advisory Board provides strategic oversight and professional guidance to ensure JDF Public Safety remains at the forefront of community security. Board members bring specialized knowledge in security operations, law enforcement, technology, and community leadership to help shape our policies, programs, and strategic direction.
