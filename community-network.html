Community and partners

ONE COMMUNITY. ONE SET OF STREETS.

JDF is for the entire community.

Families, congregations, schools and businesses move as one population through one set of streets. That is what makes shared coverage work.

Get in touch Donate

Who we serve

Everyone who walks these streets

Nobody signs a contract and nobody receives an invoice. The community funds the work together, and the whole community is protected.

Families

Children walking to school, families shopping for groceries, neighbors commuting to work.

Congregations

A unit outside as services begin, and supplemental coverage on Shabbat and the High Holidays.

Schools

Certified K-9 teams sweep schools, and patrol covers the walk to and from campus.

Businesses

Bicycle ambassadors and marked units keep a steady presence on foot-traffic streets.

For synagogues, schools and organizations

How your institution gets involved

Step 1

Get in touchTell us who you are and where you are. Contact JDF or call (213) 260-0061.

Step 2

Share your calendarServices, school hours and events. Ask about K-9 sweeps and special event coverage.

Step 3

Pass on the hotlineShare the Public Safety Hotline with your members, and remind them that 911 comes first.

SHARED COVERAGE WORKS BECAUSE IT IS SHARED

Institutions that each hire one guard for one door get no shared patrol, canine or dispatch. Pooling support behind one public safety nonprofit covers the streets between every building.
