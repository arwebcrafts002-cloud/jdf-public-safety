Funding Frameworks

SECURITY GRANT PROGRAMS & ENDOWMENTS

JDF Public Safety provides technical guidance, vulnerability assessments, and operational implementation for major public and private security funding streams.

FEMA NSGP Grants

Federal Nonprofit Security Grant Program funding physical target hardening, perimeter access controls, security cameras, and contracted security personnel.

California State CSNSGP

State-level security funding administered by Cal OES for California nonprofit institutions at risk of hate-motivated violence and property damage.

Foundation Endowments

Philanthropic family foundations and community trust funds supporting ongoing patrol coverage, tactical technology, and emergency preparedness.

Corporate Underwriting

Direct institutional and corporate sponsorships providing dedicated patrol interceptor vehicles, LPR camera nodes, and rapid response units.

Grant Services

HOW JDF SUPPORTS INSTITUTIONAL APPLICANTS

From initial physical vulnerability assessments through post-award security deployment, JDF provides end-to-end institutional support.

Physical Vulnerability Assessments

Professional on-site security audits identifying perimeter vulnerabilities, choke points, and target-hardening priorities required for grant eligibility.

Equipment & Technology Specs

Formulating compliant equipment specifications aligned with FEMA Allowable Equipment Lists (AEL), including surveillance, LPR, and access control.

Operational Deployment

Seamless execution of funded security operations, integrating institution facilities into JDF 24/7 dispatch monitoring and dedicated patrol routes.
