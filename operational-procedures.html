Common questions

Who to call, and when

Should I call JDF or 911?

911, every time there is an emergency or a crime in progress. Call the JDF hotline at (213) 260-0061 after that.

Does JDF replace the police?

No. JDF complements law enforcement and never replaces it. LAPD leads every criminal matter.

Who answers the hotline?

Dispatchers who know the neighborhood.

It is not urgent, but something did not feel right. What should I do?

Tell us. Use the Report a Concern form or call the hotline. Do not use the online form for anything urgent.

Is JDF a private security company?

No. JDF is a public safety nonprofit. Nobody signs a contract and nobody receives an invoice. Armed response is provided by a licensed California Private Patrol Operator.

SEE SOMETHING? SAY SOMETHING.

Emergencies go to 911 first. For everything else, the Public Safety Hotline and the online report form are open to the whole community.
