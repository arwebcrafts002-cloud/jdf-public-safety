Questions

Before you give

Where does my gift go?

To patrol, K-9 and dispatch operations in Zone 1, Pico-Robertson and Beverlywood, and to the vehicles, communications and equipment those teams need.

Is JDF a private security company?

No. JDF is a public safety nonprofit. Nobody signs a contract and nobody receives an invoice.

Does JDF replace the police?

No. 911 comes first. Every criminal matter is escalated immediately to 911 and LAPD.

Can I sponsor something specific?

Yes. Capabilities such as patrol vehicles, the Mobile Command Center, communications and the bicycle ambassador program can be sponsored by name. Call (213) 260-0061 or write to info@jdfpublicsafety.org.

Can I give anonymously or dedicate my gift?

Yes to both. You choose on the donation page.

YOUR SUPPORT. OUR STRENGTH.

Every contribution puts patrol, K-9 and dispatch hours on the street in Zone 1.
