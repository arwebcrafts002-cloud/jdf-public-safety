Questions

About the Founders Club

Can I be an anonymous founder?

Yes. Your plaque will read "Anonymous founder" with your seat number, and your name is never published. You still receive everything a founder receives.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

[Confirm with JDF's accountant: when a donor receives merchandise, the deductible amount is the gift minus the fair market value of the items, and gifts over $75 need that stated on the receipt. Offer a "no merch, make my whole gift count" checkbox.]

When does the merch ship?

Items are produced in batches after your gift is completed. We will email you when yours ships. Founder coins are struck once, with seat numbers, so they take longest.

What happens after the 20 seats are taken?

The Founders Club closes and is never reopened. Every other giving level stays open, and all of them fund the same patrol, K-9 and dispatch work.

Can a business or a family take a seat?

Yes. One seat per household or business, and the plaque can carry a family or business name.
