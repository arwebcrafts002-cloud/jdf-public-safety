Why we train

PRESENCE OVER REACTION

Deterrence costs less than response. A community that can spot surveillance early, knows who to call, and knows how to protect itself is harder to target. JDF patrols the street. Training prepares the people who walk it.

Questions

Before you sign up

Who leads the training?

Current and former special forces operators.

Who can take part?

Training is for community members and JDF personnel alike. There are dedicated sessions for kids and for women.

What can I learn?

Martial arts, practical self-defense, awareness (spotting surveillance and threats early), and responsible firearms handling and safety.

How do I sign up or find the schedule?

Use the contact page or call (213) 260-0061 and tell us which training you are interested in. We will reply with upcoming sessions.

What should I do if I see something concerning?

For an emergency or a crime in progress, call 911 first. Then call the JDF Public Safety Hotline at (213) 260-0061, or report a concern online.
