Who we are

A PUBLIC SAFETY NONPROFIT.

JDF is not a private security company.

Patrol units cover every street from the I-10 Freeway to Olympic Boulevard, between La Cienega and Roxbury, day and night. Nobody signs a contract and nobody receives an invoice: the community funds the work together, and the whole community is protected.

Donate Now What We Do
24/7 On the street, already in position

Armed patrol and dispatch every day and every night, with medical training to treat people until help arrives.

911 Comes first, always

Every criminal matter is escalated immediately to 911 and LAPD. JDF complements law enforcement and never replaces it.

0 Contracts or invoices

What we stand for

Mission, vision, and key values

Mission

To protect lives, deter crime, and strengthen the resilience of the communities we serve through visible, professional, and coordinated public safety operations that complement the work of law enforcement rather than attempting to replace it.

Vision

To be recognized as one of the leading nonprofit community protection organizations in the United States: continuously staffed, professionally accountable, and trusted equally by the residents we protect and by the law enforcement agencies and public agencies we work alongside every day.

Key values
  • Presence over reactionDeterrence costs less than response
  • Coordination, never substitutionLAPD leads every criminal matter
  • Accountability to the donorEach dollar traces to an hour of coverage
  • Professional standardsWithout exception

Why now

Three developments reached this neighborhood at once

The street a child walks to school is the street JDF was built to cover.

THREAT SEVERITY RISING

Antisemitic incidents hit record levels in 2024. Although the 2025 count fell, violent incidents did not.

POLICE STRENGTH AT A GENERATION LOW

Sworn LAPD strength dropped to its lowest point in a generation, spread across the entire city.

INFORMAL PROTECTION IS FRAGMENTED

A volunteer at the door and a guard hired for the holidays protect buildings, not people. They do not cover the four blocks between a synagogue and a family home.
