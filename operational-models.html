How We Protect

OPERATIONAL SECURITY MODEL

Patrol Operations & Coordinated Deterrence

Our patrol teams provide visible, coordinated security presence across Jewish institutions, schools, and community locations throughout Greater Los Angeles.

Our Framework Report a Concern

Uniformed Patrols

High-visibility uniformed public safety officers providing proactive physical deterrence and continuous neighborhood vigilance.

Mobile & Stationary

Strategic deployment of mobile interceptor units paired with stationary security posts at schools and synagogues.

Randomized Routes

Unpredictable schedule intervals and variable patrol routes designed to deter pre-operational surveillance.

Rapid Response

Integrated dispatch coordination enabling swift officer mobilization and immediate law enforcement liaison.

Our Framework

THE 6 PILLARS OF OPERATION

JDF Public Safety's proactive, multi-layered approach that integrates people, technology, and partnerships to create safer communities for all.

Threat Intelligence

Monitoring, analysis, and risk assessment to identify and understand potential threats before they materialize.

Security Teams

Trained professionals for every environment, providing visible deterrence and rapid response capabilities.

Patrol Operations

Visible presence. Rapid response. Coordinated patrol routes covering our entire service area.

Technology Systems

Advanced tools for early detection, including surveillance systems, license plate recognition, and real-time alerts.

Law Enforcement Coordination

Building together for stronger protection through active partnerships with local law enforcement agencies.

Community Safety
