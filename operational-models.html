The landscape

Every alternative covers a piece. JDF covers the neighborhood.

Volunteer security groups

Covers: The inside of a building during a service.
Misses: The street. They are unarmed and site-specific.

National coordination bodies

Covers: Intelligence, training and agency liaison.
Misses: Any patrol. They are advisory only.

Institutional guard contracts

Covers: One address, during opening hours.
Misses: Everything between buildings.

Commercial patrol companies

Covers: Routes for subscribing clients.
Misses: Non-subscribers, and canine. Coverage is as patchy as the subscription map.

Doing nothing is the true incumbent: an alarm after a break-in, a camera nobody monitors, a WhatsApp group that reports after the fact.

Working with law enforcement

COORDINATION, NEVER SUBSTITUTION

LAPD leads every criminal matter. JDF adds what a citywide force cannot give one neighborhood: dedicated, continuous street presence, and coverage between buildings, not just at addresses.

  • Dedicated, continuous street presence
  • Coverage between buildings, not just at addresses
  • Coordinates with, never replaces, LAPD
