Our programs

Six programs, one neighborhood

Select a program to see what it does on the street.

01 / 06

Armed patrols

Armed, marked and unmarked units are the backbone. A unit outside a synagogue as Friday services begin tells anyone watching that the location is protected, before anything happens. Motorcycle units reach crowded streets four wheels cannot.

Marked unitsUnmarked unitsMotorcycle unitsLicensed CA Private Patrol Operator
02 / 06

K-9 explosive detection

Certified canine teams sweep synagogues, schools and community venues. 80 explosive detection sweeps completed. Commercial patrol companies carry no canine.

SynagoguesSchoolsCommunity venues
03 / 06

Bicycle ambassadors

Friendly, approachable and fast through pedestrian areas. Ambassadors are the daily face of JDF on foot-traffic streets and a constant, reassuring presence.

Foot-traffic streetsApproachableDaily presence
04 / 06

24/7 dispatch and hotline

The Public Safety Hotline, (213) 260-0061, is answered by dispatchers who know the neighborhood, with immediate escalation to 911 and LAPD. 911 comes first. JDF complements law enforcement and never replaces it.

(213) 260-0061Immediate 911 escalationIntel and threat analysis
05 / 06

Shabbat and holiday coverage

Risk here follows the calendar. JDF adds supplemental units on 52 Shabbat days and 14 High Holiday and elevated-security days every year, and covers community events.

66 days a year5.2 units at peakSpecial event coverage
06 / 06

Community training

Training a stronger, more prepared community, led by current and former special forces operators: self-defense, martial arts including programs for kids and for women, awareness, and firearms safety.

Self-defenseKids and womenAwarenessFirearms safety
